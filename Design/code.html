Author's Dispatch

Why I spent ten years recording these domestic field notes.

Like many men of my generation, I entered fatherhood convinced that financial provision and sheer physical attendance would be sufficient. I was wrong.

Children do not measure safety in square footage or balanced spreadsheets. They measure it in the steady frequency of your eyes when they enter the room, the softness of your voice during their most humiliating failures, and the uncompromising boundaries that give their world predictable edges.

I wrote The School of Fathers not from an ivory tower of academic perfection, but from the messy trenches of domestic trial and error. This book is a distillation of what actually withstands the storms of youth.

Marcus V. Hayes Autumn 2024
